# Home (overrides MASTER)

Job: tell the farmer what matters on their farm today and get them to the right tool in one tap.
Primary action: the top item in "Today on your farm". Signature moment: the living-sky weather card (the only ambient animation in the app). Six equal tiles are not allowed: 3 primary tools (Pest photo, Weather, Ask) are large, the rest compact. One "Read this screen" button instead of a speaker per tile.
